Fuel for +8 to +15 kg

Weekly weigh-in

Same day, every week: Monday morning, after the toilet, before breakfast.

Sample training day

About 3,500 kcal at 63 kg. Eat every 3 hours and scale portions as his weight rises.

  • Breakfast (~800) 3–4 eggs on 3 slices of toast with butter, a big bowl of porridge made with whole milk and a banana, a glass of orange juice.
  • Mid-morning (~350) Greek yoghurt with granola and honey, plus a handful of nuts.
  • Lunch (~800) Chicken or tuna wrap or sandwich with cheese, a big portion of rice or pasta, salad with olive oil, a piece of fruit.
  • Around training (~500) Before: peanut butter bagel and a banana. After, within an hour: 500 ml chocolate milk and a sandwich, or a protein shake blended with oats and a banana.
  • Dinner (~800) 150–200 g beef, chicken or salmon, a double portion of potatoes, rice or pasta, vegetables, olive oil or butter.
  • Before bed (~300) Cereal with whole milk, or cottage cheese on toast.

Recovery days (Wednesday and Sunday): eat the same amount. Muscle and bone grow while he rests.

Easy extra calories

  • Whole milk, not skimmed. Add a glass to every meal.
  • Smoothie: 300 ml milk, oats, banana, peanut butter, honey (about 700 kcal).
  • Olive oil or butter on rice, pasta, potatoes and vegetables.
  • Nut butter, dried fruit, cheese and crackers, trail mix as snacks.
  • Never skip breakfast. If appetite is low, drink calories.

Game day and hydration

  • Friday dinner: extra pasta, rice or potatoes with a normal portion of chicken or fish. Nothing new or heavy.
  • Saturday breakfast (3 hours before): porridge, toast, eggs and fruit.
  • 1 hour before: banana or rice cakes with honey.
  • Half time: water and orange slices.
  • Within an hour after: chocolate milk and a sandwich, then a big dinner.
  • Water: 2.5–3.5 litres on training and game days. Pale yellow urine means he is hydrated.
  • Sleep: 9 hours. This is when he repairs and grows.

Keep it safe

  • Food first. A protein shake is fine for convenience.
  • No creatine, mass-gainer powders, pre-workouts or testosterone boosters. These are not for under-18s.
  • He is still growing taller, so some weight gain will be height.
  • See a GP or sports dietitian before starting, and if his appetite, energy or weight changes sharply.

Test days (weeks 1, 6, 12)

  • 10m and 30m sprint, 5-10-5 shuttle
  • Vertical jump and standing broad jump
  • Plank hold, pull-up max reps
  • Body weight and height (he is still growing)

Coach's rules for a 15-year-old

  • Technique first. A coach or experienced adult watches every main lift.
  • No 1-rep max tests. Add weight only when every rep is clean and the last rep felt like 2 left in the tank.
  • Warm up 10 minutes: jog, hip and shoulder mobility, bodyweight squats, 2 light ramp-up sets.
  • Rest 2–3 min between heavy sets, 60–90 s on accessories, full recovery between sprints.
  • Eat enough: protein at every meal, carbs around training, aim for steady weight gain while height grows. Sleep 9 hours.
  • Sharp joint or growth-plate pain (knee, heel, hip) means stop and see a physio.
